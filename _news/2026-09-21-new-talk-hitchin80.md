I am participating at the [Geometry conference in honour of Nigel Hitchin on the occasion of his 80th birthday](https://www.icmat.es/RT/2026/HNLTT/hitchin80.php) at [ICMAT](https://www.icmat.es/es/), where I gave a talk titled _Classification of very stable Higgs bundles_. We also [gave Nigel a present](https://www.icmat.es/news/21-09-26-en/) consisting of 3d-printed sculptures of the [Bruhat graphs](https://hausel.ista.ac.at/~thausel/zoo/gkm/) of the type A2, B2 and G2 coroot lattices, which are related (among many others) to the nilpotent cone in the Hitchin system as explained in the talk (see also [this work](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.01289)). This present was joint work with [Tamás Hausel](https://hausel.ista.ac.at/~thausel/tamas-hausel/). I will write an explanation in my projects page soon, including more pictures of the present. Check [my research page](/publications) more information, including the talk slides.
